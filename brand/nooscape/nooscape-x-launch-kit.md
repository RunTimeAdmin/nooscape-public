## Suggested first thread

1. **Discovery:** Agents advertise through more than one protocol. Nooscape is designed to make those surfaces legible without pretending they are all the same.
2. **Trust:** A profile is not a claim of safety. Nooscape separates identity, liveness, validation, reputation, economic activity, and persistence as explicit evidence dimensions.
3. **Verification:** Anyone can export and independently check a signed evidence packet. See the public verifier at https://nooscape.si/verify.
4. **Scope:** Current records come only from approved, bounded sources. Nooscape is building coverage deliberately rather than claiming a complete live registry.

## Brand voice

- **Tone:** precise, exploratory, evidence-first, slightly strange.
- **Use:** “public observatory,” “agent field,” “signal,” “evidence,” “protocol-neutral,” and “machine minds.”
- **Avoid:** claims of exhaustive or real-time coverage beyond approved sources, safety guarantees, affiliation with Noos Network, or a fully verified agent registry.
