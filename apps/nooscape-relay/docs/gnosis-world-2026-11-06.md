# The Gnosis page (Chain Worlds, stage G)

Front end and one server read, from data that already exists. No migration, no new table, no worker step, no change to the listing rules or the gate.

**Status.** Built on 2026-11-06 on branch `feat/gnosis-extras`, with the Gnosis lights that follow it in the same pull request. It is live only once that pull request merges and deploys, behind `chain_gnosis`, which production has on since the Gnosis go-live ([the record](./gnosis-erc8004-2026-11-06.md)). "Checked" below says what was seen on a built page.

**Decided:** 2026-11-06, by the operator, in chat: "lets go fully live with gnosos once you have all you need to do it", after Gnosis went live (it had no page: `/chains/gnosis` answered 404). Read as the page the other chains have, and as groups only, the way BNB Chain's, Base's, Ethereum's and Monad's pages are ([stage A](./chain-worlds-2026-10-20.md), [stage D](./base-world-2026-10-21.md), [stage E](./ethereum-world-2026-10-27.md), [stage F](./monad-world-2026-11-01.md)), standing on the platforms of the shared fact.

**Why groups only fits Gnosis.** The directory lists 1.0 percent of the registrations the registry holds (41 of 4,179), and one host name holds most of the rest: marketplace.olas.network has 3,851 of them (92 percent), 2,000 stored and 1,851 counted past the host cap ([the flood cap](./flood-host-cap-2026-10-21.md)). 10 of the 41 listed entries still show a number for a name until the names step reads the files their links name. A sample of listed entries would say little about the chain, and a page of groups says what the registry is.

## What a visitor sees

- **The page.** `/chains/gnosis`, World 08, "The teal lantern", "A quiet registry, read in groups." Teal (`#2DD4BF`, the token of its home tab, `--ns-chain-gnosis`) with a soft violet-pink (`#F0ABFC`) for the lines. A group is a stack of three plates in the shape of the Gnosis tab's icon, an octagon. Each platform has even rounded corners, a glow from the top and a faint diagonal lattice. The route exists only in a build that has the switch, the server answers 404 for it otherwise (`FLAGGED_CLIENT_ROUTES`), and its head and its place in the sitemap are written only while the switch is on. The home tab row gets "Open the Gnosis page" after Monad's.
- **What it shows.** The three figures every chain has (ratings say "Not measured" until the ratings step reads Gnosis, [the record](./gnosis-ratings-2026-11-06.md)) and the sentence the Gnosis tab has ("The chain holds N registrations. The published listing rules put M of them in the directory; the rest are held out, not judged."). Then up to three platforms: same host, same profile link, same registering wallet, in the order the other group pages use; a platform with nothing on it is not drawn. Each platform draws up to eight groups, the ones that 50 or more registrations share (`FLEET_MIN`), largest first, counted over every registration the registry holds, listed or held out. A group's size grows with the log of its count and says nothing else.
- **What the registry gives it today.** Two groups: the host (3,851 registrations) and one registering wallet (2,000, which are the registrations stored for it; the 1,851 counted past the host cap have no wallet recorded). No group of a shared profile link meets the rule. So the page draws two formations on two platforms.
- **A group's card.** It says what the group counts ("3,851 registrations on Gnosis point their profile link at marketplace.olas.network"), what that does not show, that a group is not an agent and is not reviewed, and that groups overlap.
- **No single registration is drawn.** `CHAIN_SHOWS_ACKNOWLEDGED.gnosis` is false, as BNB Chain's, Base's, Ethereum's and Monad's are. It is the one constant to change if the operator wants a sample of listed entries; Gnosis would then also need a sample read, and `chainSample` accepts Arc and AgenC only.
- **The events.** Gnosis has no global feed. The rail says so and points at no census.
- **The census report.** The inspector of a group links it (`CHAIN_GROUP_REPORT.gnosis`). It followed the operator's counts ([the record](./gnosis-census-2026-11-06.md)).
- **The outline.** The Gnosis tab's icon was a hexagon when the chain went live. Robinhood Chain's lights and page plates are already hexagons (`fleetShape`), so on the map and on the pages two chains would have differed only by colour. Gnosis's icon, lights and plates are an octagon, which no other chain uses; a test holds the eight outlines apart.

## What is read

`liveData.chainStats` (the figures and the as-of line), `publicRecords` (the reviewed records, none for Gnosis today) and `liveData.registryFleets`, which now accepts `{ chain: "gnosis" }` as it accepts Arc, Base, Ethereum and Monad. It reads the registry rows the Gnosis pass stores and returns a shared fact, its kind and how many registrations share it, and nothing about any one registration. It answers nothing while `chain_gnosis` is off, while no Gnosis source is active, and until a walk that reached the finalized head has published. The answer is held in the directory cache like every aggregate.

## What is held by a test

- `client/src/lib/chainExplorerModel.test.ts`, "the Gnosis world": the page is on offer only while `chain_gnosis` is in the build's list, last when all five extra chains are on; it draws groups and reviewed records only, never an entry a feed or the directory lists, and nothing of another chain; the platforms stand host, link, wallet, with a plate of records after them only when one exists and no empty plate; it has no feed, sets its plates in columns, names its registry and links its census report; its colour is the token of Gnosis's tab; its words hold no score, rank or verdict. The worlds are numbered one to eight.
- `server/mapFleets.test.ts`: a Gnosis group is an octagon, words its fact "on Gnosis", shares Arc's note that groups overlap and holds no verdict word. Eight chains, eight shapes.
- `server/clientRoutes.test.ts`, `server/routeHead.test.ts` and `server/sitemap.test.ts`: the page is served, headed and listed only while its switch is on, and no other chain's switch opens it; eight chain pages carry a card and no other fixed page does; each card is a different 1200 by 630 PNG under 600 KB, and its alt text names the chain, says the formations are an illustration, carries the site's two promises and holds no figure.
- `client/src/components/home/chainTabsModel.test.ts` and `client/src/components/chain/chainComponents.test.ts`: the page link follows the switch, and the event rail words Gnosis's missing feed as Gnosis's.
- `server/registryFleets.integration.test.ts`, against a real MySQL and MariaDB: Gnosis's groups come from Gnosis's rows only, nothing is served while the switch is off, and Arc's, Base's, Ethereum's, Monad's and Gnosis's never mix. The test pastes migration 0057 by hand, as production has it, because the boot runner skips it.

## Checked

On a client build with production's switch list and `chain_gnosis`, served by a preview rig that forwards the live site's data and answers Gnosis's groups with the real ones (read once, with the pass's own code, from a scratch MariaDB 10.11 that holds the real first pass: 4,179 registrations recorded, 41 listed, 1,851 counted past the host cap), in the built-in browser at desktop width and at 375 pixels:

- The page shows two platforms and two formations (the host and the wallet), both octagons, no overlap, no horizontal overflow at 375 pixels (the document is 375 pixels wide) and no console error. The figures read 41 acknowledged entries, 0 reviewed records and "Not measured" ratings, and the sentence "The chain holds 4,179 registrations. The published listing rules put 41 of them in the directory; the rest are held out, not judged." The numbered row has Gnosis as 08.
- The share card is 1200 by 630 and 472,229 bytes, built with the site's own formation marks and its two typefaces.
- Planted changes: 26 across this page and the lights, each caught by a test: a world left out of the build's list, a page that may draw single listed entries, a group filtered under Monad's protocol, an unnamed registry, a title or number of another page, the platforms in another order, a group drawn as a star, worded as Monad's, or without Arc's note, a page the server does not serve behind its switch, a sitemap line left out, Monad's card file or title, a home link to Monad's page, a rail with Monad's words, Monad's colour, and a groups read that refuses Gnosis.
- Not run on a real server: the switch-off case (no page, no sitemap line, no head). The route, head, sitemap and fleets tests hold it.

## Not in this stage

- **A census report and claims.** The operator asked for both on 2026-11-06 ("do the census report and claims"). Claims are built behind their own switch, off ([the record](./gnosis-claims-2026-11-06.md)); the census report followed ([the record](./gnosis-census-2026-11-06.md)).
- **A decision for the operator: names.** The page names the host and shortens the wallet, as the other group pages do: marketplace.olas.network (3,851) and 0x6e8f...d2f3 (2,000). The groups are described neutrally on every card, and the names are public facts, but it is the operator's call whether Gnosis's page should hold back what a census would.
- **A decision for the operator: the title, the words, the colours and the outline.** "The teal lantern" and "A quiet registry, read in groups." are the page's name and line, chosen here in the manner of the other worlds. The teal is the tab's own token; the violet-pink, the lattice and the octagon are choices made here (the octagon replaces the hexagon the tab had, for the reason above). They are one constant each (`CHAIN_WORLDS.gnosis`, `chainWorld.css`, `EXTRA_ICON.gnosis`) and the card carries them.
- **The listing rules** are Gnosis's own (`GNOSIS_LISTING_RULES`), set when the chain was added.
