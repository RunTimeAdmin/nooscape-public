# The Celo page (Chain Worlds, stage H)

Front end and one server read, from data that already exists. No migration, no new table, no worker step, no change to the listing rules or the gate.

**Status.** Built on 2026-11-20 on branch `feat/celo-page`, with the Celo lights that follow it in the same pull request. It is live only once that pull request merges and deploys, behind `chain_celo`, which production has on since the Celo go-live ([the record](./celo-erc8004-2026-11-20.md)). "Checked" below says what was seen on a built page.

**Decided:** 2026-11-20, by the operator, in chat: "all in order", on my list of what Celo still lacked after it went live, where the page, the card and the lights came third (it had no page: `/chains/celo` answered 404). Read as the page the other chains have, and as groups only, the way BNB Chain's, Base's, Ethereum's, Monad's and Gnosis's pages are ([stage A](./chain-worlds-2026-10-20.md), [stage D](./base-world-2026-10-21.md), [stage E](./ethereum-world-2026-10-27.md), [stage F](./monad-world-2026-11-01.md), [stage G](./gnosis-world-2026-11-06.md)), standing on the platforms of the shared fact.

**Why groups only fits Celo.** The directory lists 5.1 percent of the registrations the registry holds (517 of 10,080), and 286 of those 517 still show a number for a name until the names step reads the files their links name. Two registering wallets hold 85 percent of all registrations (6,934 and 1,630). A sample of listed entries would say little about the chain, and a page of groups says what the registry is.

## What a visitor sees

- **The page.** `/chains/celo`, World 09, "The jade compass", "A crowded registry, read in groups." Green (`#35D07F`, the token of its home tab, `--ns-chain-celo`) with a soft gold (`#FDE68A`) for the lines. A group is a stack of three plates in the shape of the Celo tab's icon, an arrowhead (a triangle with a notch in its base) pointing up. Each platform has rounded corners, a glow from the top and a faint triangular lattice (three families of lines, 26 pixels apart). The route exists only in a build that has the switch, the server answers 404 for it otherwise (`FLAGGED_CLIENT_ROUTES`), and its head and its place in the sitemap are written only while the switch is on. The home tab row gets "Open the Celo page" after Gnosis's.
- **What it shows.** The three figures every chain has (ratings say "not measured here" until the ratings step reads Celo, [the record](./celo-ratings-2026-11-20.md)) and the sentence the Celo tab has ("The chain holds N registrations. The published listing rules put M of them in the directory; the rest are held out, not judged."). Then up to three platforms: same host, same profile link, same registering wallet, in the order the other group pages use; a platform with nothing on it is not drawn. Each platform draws up to eight groups, the ones that 50 or more registrations share (`FLEET_MIN`), largest first, counted over every registration the registry holds, listed or held out. A group's size grows with the log of its count and says nothing else.
- **What the registry gives it today.** Ten groups, read on 2026-11-20 with the app's own code from Celo's 10,080 registry rows: six registering wallets (6,934, 1,630, 105, 73, 63 and 59 registrations) and four hosts (raw.githubusercontent.com 260, poulavbhowmick03.github.io 158, execution.market 56 and celo.manekiai.io 55). No group of a shared profile link meets the rule. So the page draws two platforms: four formations on the host platform and six on the wallet platform.
- **A group's card.** It says what the group counts ("260 registrations on Celo point their profile link at raw.githubusercontent.com"), what that does not show, that a group is not an agent and is not reviewed, and that groups overlap.
- **No single registration is drawn.** `CHAIN_SHOWS_ACKNOWLEDGED.celo` is false, as BNB Chain's, Base's, Ethereum's, Monad's and Gnosis's are. It is the one constant to change if the operator wants a sample of listed entries; Celo would then also need a sample read, and `chainSample` accepts Arc and AgenC only.
- **The events.** Celo has no global feed. The rail says so and points at no census.
- **The census report.** The inspector of a group links it (`CHAIN_GROUP_REPORT.celo`). It was the next stage of the Celo work ([the record](./celo-census-2026-11-20.md)).
- **The outline.** The Celo tab's icon was a placeholder arrowhead when the chain went live. It is now the outline of the lights and the plates. It is not a triangle: the map already draws a record with no chain as a triangle, and a test holds the arrowhead apart from it as it holds every chain's outline apart.

## What is read

`liveData.chainStats` (the figures and the as-of line), `publicRecords` (the reviewed records, none for Celo today) and `liveData.registryFleets`, which now accepts `{ chain: "celo" }` as it accepts Arc, Base, Ethereum, Monad and Gnosis. It reads the registry rows the Celo pass stores and returns a shared fact, its kind and how many registrations share it, and nothing about any one registration. It answers nothing while `chain_celo` is off, while no Celo source is active, and until a walk that reached the finalized head has published. The answer is held in the directory cache like every aggregate.

## Checked

On a client build with production's switch list and `chain_celo`, served by the real server (the built app, `node dist/index.js`) against a scratch MySQL 8 that holds Celo's rows as the production database held them at about 19:37 UTC on 2026-11-20 (10,080 registry rows and 517 listed entries, a read-only dump of public chain facts), in the built-in browser at desktop width and at 375 pixels:

- The page shows two platforms and ten formations, four on the host platform (260, 158, 56 and 55 registrations) and six on the wallet platform (6,934, 1,630, 105, 73, 63 and 59), all arrowheads on the lattice, none overlapping. The plate of shared profile links is not drawn, because no link meets the rule. The figures read 517 acknowledged entries, 0 reviewed records and "Not measured" ratings, and the sentence "The chain holds 10,080 registrations. The published listing rules put 517 of them in the directory; the rest are held out, not judged." The row of worlds has Celo as 09 and its chip selected. The document had no horizontal overflow at 375 pixels (it is 375 pixels wide) and the console held no error.

- The head of `/chains/celo` carries the title "Celo registry groups | Nooscape", the canonical address, an indexing rule, and the card as `og:image` with its alt text. The card is served as `image/png` (288,005 bytes), and `/chains/celo` is in the sitemap.
- The groups the page drew are the answer of the app's own `readRegistryFleets("celo")` over those rows: 10 groups (6 wallets and 4 hosts), 10,080 recorded, none counted past a cap. `liveData.registryFleets` answers the same ten.
- The share card is 1200 by 630 and 288,005 bytes, built with the site's own formation marks drawn in the arrowhead outline and its two typefaces.
- Planted changes: 30 across this page and the lights, each caught by a test: a world left out of the build's list, a page that may draw single listed entries, a group filtered under Gnosis's protocol, an unnamed registry, the number and the title of another page, the platforms in another order, a group drawn as an octagon, worded as Gnosis's or without Arc's note, a page the server does not serve behind its switch, a sitemap line left out, another chain's card file and title in the head, a home link to another page, a rail with Gnosis's words, Gnosis's colour, the triangle of a record with no chain, an arrowhead that is not the tab's icon, lights placed first, eight lights and not five, a groups read that refuses Celo, a hero that is not handed Celo's groups or has no key entry, a record on Celo that the map still sets aside, a legend without Celo, a canvas with no arrowhead outline, a route left out of the router, an entry's page in Gnosis's colour and a plate drawn with the octagon's outline. 29 were caught by the tests as they stood. ## Not in this stage

- **A census report and claims.** Both were later stages of the Celo work ("all in order": the census, then `pay_to` and claims). The census report followed ([the record](./celo-census-2026-11-20.md)), and claims after it ([the record](./celo-claims-2026-11-20.md)), built behind their own switch, off.
- **A decision for the operator: names.** The page names the host and shortens the wallet, as the other group pages do: raw.githubusercontent.com (260) and 0xc153...7d03 (6,934). The groups are described neutrally on every card, and the names are public facts, but it is the operator's call whether Celo's page should hold back what a census would. The census report names no host, wallet or agent.
- **A decision for the operator: the title, the words, the colours and the outline.** "The jade compass" and "A crowded registry, read in groups." are the page's name and line, chosen here in the manner of the other worlds. The green is the tab's own token; the gold, the lattice and the arrowhead plates are choices made here. They are one constant each (`CHAIN_WORLDS.celo`, `chainWorld.css`, `PLATE.arrow` in `FormationMark.tsx`) and the card carries them.
- **The listing rules** are Celo's own (`CELO_LISTING_RULES`), set when the chain was added.
