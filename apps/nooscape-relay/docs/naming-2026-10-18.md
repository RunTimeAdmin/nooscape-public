# What Nooscape calls itself (2026-10-18)

The site called itself four things: a "background check" in the hero, an "open agent observatory" in the header, an "evidence-first agent atlas" in the page title and the about page, and a "public directory" on the legal pages. The words promise different things, and the hero's "Check any agent before you trust it" promised a verdict that, for nearly every agent, the site can only answer with "listed, not reviewed". The operator asked for one consistent set, starting from a draft written outside the repository. This record is what was built from it, what was changed from the draft and why, and what waits.

## The two phrases

- **What it is: an open agent observatory.** Nooscape watches registries it does not run (three on Solana, the ERC-8004 registries on Robinhood Chain and BNB Chain), keeps the evidence it can read, and labels each record by how much has been checked.
- **What it is for: the evidence layer for agent trust.** What someone needs to decide for themselves whether an agent's record holds up, with each record labeled by how much of it has been checked. Nooscape does not decide who to trust.

## The limits the names must respect

The site has promised, in writing, "No scores for sale. No rankings for hire.", that a tier is "a level of evidence, not a score", and (on the terms) "We never take money to change a record." So no name may imply grading, scoring or selling judgment. Out as names for Nooscape: credit bureau, background check, graded, evidence-first atlas. `client/src/naming.test.ts` holds this to the words a visitor reads (the client, the page shell, the manifest and the server's page heads), and holds the two promises word for word.

Two rules from the draft were changed on purpose:
- The draft banned the words "score" and "ranking" except in the two promises. The site's own disclaimers use them in the negative ("not a score", "Light is evidence, not a score"), and so did the draft's own legal paragraph. Negations stay; claims do not.
- The draft banned "verified" as a claim about an agent. It is also a precise tier name ("Domain-control verified", one record) and the name of the packet verifier. It stays only in those exact uses, and not as a general claim about an agent.

## What the draft said that was not true, and what replaced it

- "The evidence behind every record": only Robinhood has settlement and rating ledgers. Solana and BNB records carry the registry's own facts and nothing more. The hero says "the evidence it can read".
- "Of roughly 240,000 agents, 76 reviewed, 99.8 percent": the live figures are about 250,000 acknowledged across all sources and about 32,500 registered on-chain, so 99.8 percent fits only the second. Not used.
- "Republishes records from public agent registries": 51 of the 76 reviewed records come from catalogs and agent cards. The terms say "read from approved sources", which is accurate.
- "Evidence those registries do not keep": the ERC-8004 reputation registry keeps ratings on-chain. Nooscape reads and keeps them as signed observations.

## Not done, and why

- **`/terms`, applied 2026-10-18.** The operator's legal assistant signed off on the old `/terms` wording on 2026-10-17, and the operator's legal review of 2026-10-18 found the new wording fine to merge. The page's "What Nooscape is" and the description the server writes for it now say "an open agent observatory" where they said "a public directory", and `naming.test.ts` fails if "public directory" appears anywhere. The review made one change, applied here: "the level of evidence on record for it", not "checked behind it", which could read as vouching for the evidence. The same review listed what the Terms still lack (the operator named as a party, a limit of liability, agreement by use, acceptable use, a line on third-party content, governing law, and boilerplate on changes, severability and ownership) and asked for two additions to Privacy (how long rate-limit logs are kept and who the controller is); none of that is in this change, and `/disputes` and `/privacy` are unchanged.
- **The social card image.** Done 2026-10-18 (see below). It carried the words "Evidence-first agent atlas" in the picture itself.
